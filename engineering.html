Research & innovation

The problems behind the work.

Two public contributions to durability and vehicle structures, followed by the research that helped build my engineering foundation.

Co-authored paper2020

Bringing road loads
into the lab.

Vector Load Simplified Duty Cycle for Lower Control Arm

SAE Technical Paper 2020-01-1058
DOI: 10.4271/2020-01-1058

Read the SAE paper (opens in a new tab)
The challenge
A lower control arm sees loads from several directions on the road. Reproducing the full history in a laboratory can be complex; simplifying it risks losing the behavior that matters.
The published approach
The paper uses finite element analysis and proving-ground loads to derive a simplified loading profile and its direction together.
The contribution & example
I am a co-author of this work. The paper demonstrates the method on a lower control arm with a statically responsive model, connecting real loading conditions with a simpler durability test.
Co-invented patent2024

Supporting fuel tanks
as a system.

Vehicle fuel storage mounting system

US Patent 12,145,435 B2
Granted November 19, 2024

Explore the patent (opens in a new tab)
The challenge
A fuel-tank assembly must stay secure as its vehicle moves and flexes. The patent addresses tank support, excessive movement, and impact absorption together.
The documented design
A vertical structure carries multiple fuel tanks and connects to brackets outside the chassis rails. Its supports transfer the assembly’s weight and applied forces into the vehicle frame.
The contribution & record
I am a co-inventor, alongside Frederik Tjonneland and Jared Trauernicht. The granted patent documents the mounting design and its intended mechanical behavior.
